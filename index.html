02 / Follow-up with boundaries

Keep the conversation moving

Review the workflow stages below. In deployment, messages require approved content, valid consent, connected vendors and staff ownership. STOP requests end marketing follow-up.

0 of 6 stages
Inquiry received

Answer the operational question

Provide practice-approved service information and offer a consultation request. Route pricing that needs an assessment to staff rather than inventing a quote.

Text preview: "Thanks for reaching out. Would you like to explore our consultation options?"
Missed call

Offer a permitted text-back

Check consent and messaging rules before sending: "Thanks for contacting our practice. Would you like help requesting a consultation?"

Text preview: "Sorry we missed your call. Would you like help finding a consultation time? Reply STOP to opt out."
Request submitted

Confirm only after calendar acceptance

Check actual availability, booking policies and provider requirements. Send confirmation only after the scheduling system returns a valid reservation.

Text preview: "Your consultation is confirmed for [accepted date and time]. View details through our secure scheduling portal."
Before the visit

Make confirmation or rescheduling easy

Use the practice's approved timing and a secure scheduling link. Do not include sensitive treatment details in routine text messages.

Text preview: "A reminder about your upcoming consultation. Confirm or request a new time through your scheduling link."
After the visit

Keep clinical instructions with the care team

Share only provider-approved aftercare through an approved channel. Symptoms and treatment questions go to qualified staff, not automated advice.

Text preview: "Thank you for visiting. Your care team has shared your approved instructions through the secure portal. Contact the practice with questions."
Return visit

Follow the provider's interval and consent

Use the care team's recommended follow-up interval, suppress opted-out contacts and stop automation when a person needs help.

Text preview: "Your care team has recommended a follow-up. Would you like help requesting a time? Reply STOP to opt out."
